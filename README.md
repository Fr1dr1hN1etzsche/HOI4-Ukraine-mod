# HOI4-Ukraine-mod
This mod was created with immense enthusiasm to give Ukraine a deep, engaging, and well-developed mechanic in Hearts of Iron IV, featuring unique development paths and historical (or alternate-historical) immersion. Unfortunately, due to a lack of free time and personal circumstances, I am unable to bring this project to a final release. 

I am publishing this mod in the open domain with one main goal: to find enthusiasts, modders, or a dedicated team willing to take these foundations, polish them, and release a fully realized mod. 

The core idea of the mod expands the gameplay for Ukraine, adding new political paths—including democratic, nationalist, communist, and monarchist branches—along with unique national spirits and dynamic interactions with neighboring countries on the brink of World War II. 

A solid foundation has already been laid out. The basic skeleton of the focus tree is complete, particularly the political and industrial branches. Several historical leaders, generals, and advisors have been integrated, and the map has been adjusted with new victory points and resource distributions. I have also written several initial story events to set the tone, and uploaded some custom portraits and focus icons, though vanilla placeholders are currently used in some spots.

However, there is still significant work required to cross the finish line. The national focuses and spirits need rigorous balancing against the vanilla game to ensure Ukraine is neither too weak nor overpowered. Localization needs to be finished, as some focus descriptions and events still show internal code tags. Additionally, the remaining generals need quality portraits, custom focus icons must be drawn, and late-game content for the post-1941 period needs to be written. There are also a few script bugs and logic issues during specific focus selections that require debugging.

You are completely free to take the code, scripts, graphics, and any other assets from this mod to continue the work. You do not need to ask for my permission to create a fork or use these files—just take them and create something great! If you do release a finished mod based on these files, I only ask that you include me in the credits as the original creator. 

If you have any questions about the mod's structure or want to discuss transferring the Steam Workshop page, feel free to reach out to me via Discord or Steam. 

Good luck to the future developer! I hope to open the Steam Workshop one day and see this mod at the top. Glory to Ukraine!
